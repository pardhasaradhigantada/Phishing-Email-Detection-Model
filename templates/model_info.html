Feature Space & Extraction Architecture

Text Feature Extraction

TF-IDF Vectorization over normalized email bodies and subject headers using unigrams and bigrams. Sublinear term-frequency scaling is applied to attenuate the impact of frequent terms.

• N-Gram Range: (1, 2)
• Stop Words: Standard English dictionary pruned
• Sublinear TF: Enabled (replace tf with 1 + log(tf))
• Vocabulary Capacity: 5,000 maximum active features
